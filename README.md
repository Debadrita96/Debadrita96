## About Me

I'm a master's student and ML engineer specialising in **deep learning for medical imaging** — with a focus on CT reconstruction, transformer-based generative models, and multi-modal clinical AI.

My thesis investigates **motion artifact compensation in CT** using autoregressive transformer models (RandAR/LLaMAGen-style), comparing token ordering strategies (Hilbert curve vs. raster scan) and positional encoding designs (2D vs. 1D RoPE), evaluated against a comprehensive suite of image quality metrics.

Alongside my studies I work as a **service engineer at Med AI Solutions GmbH** and previously contributed to research at University Medical Center Mainz and project work at FAU's Pattern Recognition Lab.

## 🔬 Current Focus

- **MSc Thesis** — CT motion artifact compensation with autoregressive transformers (RandAR / LLaMAGen)
- Evaluating image quality across PSNR, SSIM, MS-SSIM, FSIM, VIFp, LPIPS, HFEN, GMSD
- Exploring token ordering and rotary positional embeddings for 2D medical image generation
